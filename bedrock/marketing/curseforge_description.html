The Darkin Blade – Aatrox Addon

Wield Aatrox's living greatsword in Minecraft Bedrock.

The Darkin Blade adds a fully animated 3D greatsword with a complete skill kit inspired by Aatrox from League of Legends: a three-part Q, a dash, fiery chains, a passive and the World Ender transformation with 3D demon wings, horns and a zone of destruction. Everything is made in a Minecraft pixel art style.

✅ Bedrock 1.21.90+ ✅ No experiments needed ✅ Mobile, PC and Console

⚔️ Features

  • 3D blade with a glowing lava core and a blinking Darkin eye, held correctly in first and third person
  • Smooth player animations for every skill: wind-up, swing, impact, follow-through and recovery
  • World Ender form: 3D flapping bat-like wings, horns and a bigger blade, plus a burning zone that follows you
  • 29 pixel art particles: crescent slashes, shockwaves, cracked ground, fire pillars, red lightning, screaming souls, blood orbs and more
  • Screen shake and a red flash on big hits and on transformation
  • On-screen guide and cooldown bar, works on mobile, PC and console

🎮 Controls & Skills

Input Skill Effect
Right-click / tap Q – The Darkin Blade Three slashes (4 s to recast). Rune tiles show the area; the orange edge is the sweet spot: x1.6 damage, knock-up and stun. The third cast slams the ground.
Sprint + attack E – Umbral Dash Dash where you look, leaving afterimages.
Sneak + right-click W – Infernal Chains Throw a fiery chain. If the target doesn't escape the binding circle in 1.5 s, it is pulled back and stunned.
Sneak + jump R – World Ender A shockwave knocks back and fears enemies, then you transform for 10 s: +30% skill damage, +50% healing, Speed II, Strength I.
Normal attack Passive – Deathbringer Stance Every 8 s, your hit explodes for 8% of the target's max health and heals you.

All damage dealt with the blade has 15% lifesteal. The bar above the hotbar shows which skill your next right-click will cast and every cooldown. Type /scriptevent aatrox:help to see the guide again.

🔥 World Ender Empowerment

  • World Ender zone (6-block radius) follows you: enemies inside take damage every second and are slowed
  • Q: 30% bigger area with fire pillars along the slash
  • E: 40% longer dash, half cooldown and a burning trail
  • W: throws 3 chains in a fan
  • Passive recharges twice as fast

🗡️ How to Get the Blade

  • Command: /give @s aatrox:darkin_blade
  • Crafting (shapeless): Netherite Sword + Nether Star + Block of Redstone
  • Creative inventory: Equipment → Swords

📥 Installation

  1. Download AatroxDarkinBlade.mcaddon and open it. Minecraft imports everything automatically.
  2. Create or edit a world → Behavior Packs → activate Aatrox - The Darkin Blade (BP) (the resource pack is added automatically).
  3. No experimental toggles are needed.

Updating? Remove the old version from your world first, then activate the new one.

⚙️ Customization

Every value (damage, cooldowns, ranges, PvP on/off, World Ender bonuses) can be changed in scripts/config.js inside the behavior pack.
