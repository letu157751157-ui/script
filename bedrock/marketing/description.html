THE DARKIN BLADE

Wield Aatrox's living greatsword in Minecraft Bedrock.
Bedrock 1.21.90+No experimentsMobile · PC · Console

About

The Darkin Blade adds a fully animated 3D greatsword with a complete skill kit inspired by Aatrox from League of Legends: three-part Q, dash, chains, a passive and the World Ender transformation with 3D demon wings, horns and a zone of destruction. Everything is made in a Minecraft pixel art style: 29 custom particles, glowing lava veins, and a Darkin eye on the blade that blinks.

Features

  • 3D blade with a glowing lava core and a blinking Darkin eye, held correctly in first and third person.
  • Smooth player animations for every skill: wind-up, swing, impact, follow-through and recovery.
  • World Ender form: 3D flapping bat-like wings, horns and a bigger blade, plus a burning zone that follows you.
  • 29 pixel art particles: crescent slashes, shockwaves, cracked ground, fire pillars, red lightning, screaming souls, blood orbs and more.
  • Screen shake and a red flash on big hits and on transformation.
  • Controls work on mobile, PC and console, with an on-screen guide and cooldown bar.

Controls & Skills

InputSkillEffect
Right-click / tapQ – The Darkin BladeThree slashes (4 s to recast). Rune tiles show the area; the orange edge is the sweet spot: ×1.6 damage, knock-up and stun. The third cast slams the ground.
Sprint + attackE – Umbral DashDash where you look, leaving afterimages.
Sneak + right-clickW – Infernal ChainsThrow a fiery chain. If the target doesn't escape the binding circle in 1.5 s, it is pulled back and stunned.
Sneak + JumpR – World EnderShockwave that knocks back and fears enemies, then transform for 10 s: +30% skill damage, +50% healing, Speed II, Strength I.
Normal attackPassive – Deathbringer StanceEvery 8 s, your hit explodes for 8% of the target's max health and heals you.

All damage dealt with the blade has 15% lifesteal. The bar above the hotbar shows which skill your next right-click will cast and every cooldown. Type /scriptevent aatrox:help to show the guide again.

World Ender Empowerment

  • World Ender zone (6-block radius) follows you: enemies inside take damage every second and are slowed.
  • Q: 30% bigger area with fire pillars along the slash.
  • E: 40% longer dash, half cooldown, and a burning trail.
  • W: throws 3 chains in a fan.
  • Passive recharges twice as fast.

How to Get the Blade

  • Command: /give @s aatrox:darkin_blade
  • Crafting (shapeless): Netherite Sword + Nether Star + Block of Redstone
  • Creative inventory: Equipment → Swords

Installation

  1. Download AatroxDarkinBlade.mcaddon and open it. Minecraft imports everything automatically.
  2. Create or edit a world → Behavior Packs → activate Aatrox - The Darkin Blade (BP) (the resource pack is added automatically).
  3. No experimental toggles are needed.

Updating? Remove the old version from your world first, then activate the new one.

Customization

Every value (damage, cooldowns, ranges, PvP on/off, World Ender bonuses) lives in scripts/config.js in the behavior pack.
